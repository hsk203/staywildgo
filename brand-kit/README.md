# StayWildGo Brand Kit

## Brand direction
Outdoor adventure / nature exploration. The emblem combines sun, mountain peaks, evergreen trees and a winding river/trail.

## Color palette
- Forest Green: #0B4F35
- Nature Green: #2E7D4B
- River Blue: #159BCB
- Adventure Orange: #F57C00
- Sun Yellow: #FFC107
- White: #FFFFFF

## Included
- SVG/: editable, scalable vector master files (horizontal, stacked, icon; color and white variants).
- PNG/: transparent PNG exports in multiple sizes.
- Favicon/: 16, 32, 48, 64, 96, 180, 192 and 512 px PNGs plus favicon.ico.

## Website use
- Header: SVG/staywildgo-horizontal.svg (recommended).
- Dark header: SVG/staywildgo-horizontal-white.svg.
- Social avatar: PNG/staywildgo-icon-512.png or SVG/staywildgo-icon.svg.
- Browser favicon: Favicon/favicon.ico; also use favicon-32x32.png and favicon-16x16.png.

## Notes
The SVGs are vector artwork and can be opened in Figma, Illustrator or Inkscape. PNG exports are transparent. The white variants are intended for dark backgrounds. This is a practical web brand kit, not a trademark clearance or a font-licensed commercial type package.
